## Getting Started

### What You Need to Know

The Bastion Standard Admin Portal is a secure system designed exclusively for company employees. Access is protected by multiple layers of security to keep business data safe.

### What is an Employee ID?

Your Employee ID is a unique identifier that serves as your username for the admin portal. It follows this format:

```
EMP-YYYYMMDD-XXXX
```

Example: `EMP-20260323-A1B2`

- **EMP** = Employee designation
- **YYYYMMDD** = Date your account was created
- **XXXX** = Unique 4-character code

**Important:** Your manager or owner will provide your Employee ID when you're hired. Keep it in a safe place.

## First-Time Login

When you first join Bastion Standard as an employee, you'll need to complete a one-time setup process.

### Step 1: Receive Your Employee ID

Your manager or the business owner will provide you with:
- Your Employee ID
- A link to the admin portal: `https://your-domain.com/admin/login`

### Step 2: Access the Portal

1. Go to the admin login page
2. Enter your Employee ID (the one your manager gave you)
3. Leave the password field empty on your first login
4. Click "Sign In"

### Step 3: Create Your Password

The system will detect this is your first time logging in and guide you through setup.

**Creating a Strong Password:**
- Must be at least 8 characters long
- Include uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include numbers
- Include special characters (!, @, #, $, etc.)
- Don't use common words or personal information
- Don't reuse passwords from other websites

**Example of a strong password:** `Bastion2026!Shop#`

After entering your password twice to confirm, click "Continue to Security Questions."

### Step 4: Set Up Security Questions

You'll need to select and answer 4 security questions. These are your safety net if you ever forget your password.

**How to Choose Good Security Questions:**
1. Pick questions you can answer easily but others cannot guess
2. Choose questions with answers that won't change over time
3. Use memorable, specific answers
4. Answers are case-insensitive (capitals don't matter)

**Available Questions:**
- What was the name of your first pet?
- What city were you born in?
- What is your mother's maiden name?
- What was the name of your first school?
- What is your favorite book?
- What was your childhood nickname?
- What is the name of the street you grew up on?
- What was your first car make and model?
- What is your favorite movie?
- What was the name of your first employer?
- What is your favorite food?
- What was your high school mascot?

**Important:** Your answers are encrypted and stored securely. Even system administrators cannot see your answers.

### Step 5: Complete Your Profile

In the final step, provide your employment information:

**Required Fields:**
- First Name
- Last Name

**Optional but Recommended:**
- Position (Owner, Manager, Sales Associate, etc.)
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Phone Extension

This information helps other team members contact you and appears on your admin dashboard.

### Step 6: Finish Setup

After completing all steps, click "Complete Setup." You'll be redirected to the login page with a success message. You can now log in using your Employee ID and the password you just created.

## Regular Login

After your initial setup, logging in is straightforward:

1. Go to `https://your-domain.com/admin/login`
2. Enter your Employee ID
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Sign In"

You'll be taken directly to your admin dashboard.

**Security Tips:**
- Never share your password with anyone
- Don't write down your password in an insecure place
- Always log out when you finish working
- Never leave your computer unattended while logged in

## Forgot Your Password

If you forget your password, don't worry! You can reset it using your security questions.

### Password Reset Process

1. Go to the admin login page
2. Click "Forgot your password?"
3. Enter your Employee ID
4. Click "Continue"

### Select a Security Question

You'll see the list of security questions you set up during your first login. Choose one you remember the answer to.

### Answer the Question & Create New Password

1. Type the answer to your security question
   - Spelling matters, but capitals don't (case-insensitive)
   - Answer must match exactly what you entered during setup
2. Enter your new password
3. Confirm your new password
4. Click "Reset Password"

If your answer is correct, your password will be updated immediately and you can log in with your new password.

**What if I get the answer wrong?**
- You have multiple attempts
- Try another security question if the first doesn't work
- Make sure you're spelling the answer correctly
- Contact your manager if you can't remember any answers

Your Employee ID is permanent and doesn't change. If you forget it:

### Retrieval Options

1. **Check Your Welcome Email** - Your manager should have sent your Employee ID when you were hired
2. **Ask Your Manager** - Managers have access to view all employee IDs
3. **Contact the Business Owner** - The owner can look up your Employee ID using your name or email

1. Log into the admin portal
2. Go to "Employee Management"
3. Search by employee name or email
4. View their Employee ID

## Security Questions

### What Are Security Questions?

Security questions are a backup authentication method. If you forget your password, answering one of your pre-selected questions proves your identity.

### How They Work

- You selected 4 questions during first-time setup
- Answers are encrypted and cannot be seen by anyone
- You only need to answer ONE question correctly to reset your password
- Questions cannot be changed after setup (for security reasons)

### Best Practices

**Do:**
- Choose questions with memorable answers
- Use specific, detailed answers
- Remember your exact spelling

**Don't:**
- Don't share your answers with anyone
- Don't use answers that others could easily guess
- Don't choose questions with answers that might change

### Updating Security Questions

Security questions cannot be changed through the portal (this prevents unauthorized access). If you need to update your security questions:

1. Contact your manager or the business owner
2. Verify your identity in person
3. A system administrator will reset your security questions
4. You'll need to log in and set up new questions

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### "Invalid Employee ID or Password"

**Possible Causes:**
- Typing error in Employee ID or password
- Caps Lock is on
- Extra spaces before or after your input
- Account not activated yet

**Solutions:**
- Double-check your Employee ID format
- Verify Caps Lock is off
- Copy and paste your Employee ID if possible
- Contact your manager to verify account status

#### "Account Already Set Up" Error During First Login

**Cause:** You've already completed first-time setup

**Solution:** Use the regular login with your password

#### Can't Reset Password - Security Question Answer Incorrect

**Possible Causes:**
- Spelling error in your answer
- Answer formatting is different from what you entered originally
- Trying to answer a different question than you originally set up

**Solutions:**
- Try another security question
- Check your spelling carefully
- Remember answers are case-insensitive
- Contact your manager if you can't remember any answers

#### Locked Out of Account

**Cause:** Multiple failed login attempts (security feature)

**Solution:** Wait 15 minutes and try again, or contact your manager to unlock your account

#### Session Expired

**Cause:** You were inactive for too long (security feature)

**Solution:** Simply log in again - your work is saved

## Security & Best Practices

### Keeping the System Secure

**You are responsible for:**
- Keeping your Employee ID confidential
- Creating a strong password
- Never sharing your password
- Logging out when you finish
- Not leaving your workstation unattended while logged in
- Reporting any suspicious activity

**The system protects you with:**
- Encrypted password storage
- Secure session management
- Automatic logout after inactivity
- Security question verification
- Protected database with Row Level Security

### Data Privacy

- Employee information is confidential
- Customer data must be handled according to privacy policies
- Never share business data outside the organization
- Only access information you need for your job

### Account Security Tips

1. **Use a unique password** - Don't reuse passwords from other sites
2. **Don't share credentials** - Each employee must have their own account
3. **Log out properly** - Don't just close the browser
4. **Report issues immediately** - If something seems wrong, tell your manager
5. **Update your information** - Keep your contact details current

## Appendix: For Managers

### Creating New Employee Accounts

As a manager or owner, you can create new employee accounts:

1. Log into the admin portal
2. Navigate to "Employee Management"
3. Click "Add New Employee"
4. Fill in basic information (name, position, email)
5. System automatically generates an Employee ID
6. Provide the Employee ID to the new employee
7. Instruct them to complete first-time setup

### Viewing Employee Information

- Access Employee Management section
- View list of all employees
- See Employee IDs, contact info, and positions
- Check account status (active/inactive)
- View last login times

### Handling Password Reset Requests

If an employee cannot answer their security questions:

1. Verify their identity in person (photo ID)
2. Access admin controls
3. Reset their security questions
4. Employee will need to set up new questions on next login

**Important:** Never create new passwords for employees. The system is designed so that only the employee knows their password. Reset their security questions instead and let them create a new password.
